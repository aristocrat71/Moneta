Moneta

Keep what your hand writes.

Moneta is a minimal handwriting notebook for macOS and your drawing tablet. Your ink, one floating island of tools, and nothing else.

Then paste it in your Terminal and press Enter.

v1.0.0 · universal (Apple silicon + Intel) · 8.7 MB · Free

Features

01

Ink first

Pressure-sensitive pen tuned for drawing tablets. Nothing ever sits between pen-down and ink — no lag, no ceremony.

02

One island, no bars

Every tool in a single floating pill, on keys 1–5. No menus, no toolbars, no sidebars — chrome appears on demand and disappears.

03

A library of your ink

Notebooks show live thumbnails of real pages, file into projects, and reopen exactly where you last edited. Fuzzy search on ⌘K.

04

Glass

Fade the paper away and write over whatever is behind the window — a paper, a video, a design. g g toggles it; click-through included.

05

Paper & slate

Light mode reads as paper, dark as slate, and your black ink turns bone-white on its own. This page does it too — try the toggle above.

06

Plain files, no cloud

Notebooks are plain JSON in ~/Moneta. Fully offline, no accounts, no telemetry. Export any page range to PDF, SVG, or PNG.

Named for Juno Moneta, Roman keeper of memory. Source Releases Changelog
